Inside Success trains a specific Call 2 procedure. In addition to the four dimensions, evaluate whether the rep followed it, report each step as a procedural check, and let a skipped step inform the relevant dimension band as described. Judge substance and timing, not exact wording. Every 'yes' or 'no' check needs an exact transcript timestamp and a 5-10 word verbatim excerpt from one line when the transcript can show it; when the transcript genuinely cannot show it, use 'unable_to_determine' and never penalize the rep for it.

The required Call 2 flow:
1. Recording disclosure. Near the start the rep states the call is recorded for quality, training or compliance purposes. Report yes/no.
2. Greenlight review and opening. The rep states the purpose of the call and reviews the prospect's greenlight. This should take about five minutes and normally not exceed ten unless the prospect drives the extra discussion. The rep leads; the greenlight must not become a second discovery call. Mark the greenlight segment boundaries: start_timestamp is the first mention of the greenlight, casting result or approval; end_timestamp is the moment the rep transitions to Rudy's video (or, if no video, the first pricing or offer statement). Code computes the duration. If either boundary cannot be located, set it to null. Set prospect_driven_extension true only when the prospect's own questions or storytelling clearly extended the segment.
3. Screen share during the greenlight. A transcript cannot show a screen share. Report 'unable_to_determine' unless a speaker explicitly says the screen or the letter is being shared, in which case report yes with that line.
4. Transition to Rudy's video. After the greenlight the rep moves confidently into Rudy's video without unnecessary discussion. Zoom transcripts label played media as a speaker such as 'Audio shared by <rep>' and Rudy's recorded voice often opens with a greeting like 'Hello, this is Rudy'. Report yes when those lines or the rep's explicit transition show the video played; no when the rep clearly skipped it; otherwise 'unable_to_determine'. Mark video start and end timestamps when visible.
5. Assume the sale after the video. When the video ends the rep confidently moves the prospect into the enrollment or payment process as the next action, for example by naming the first payment and how it is taken, sending the payment link, or stating which license they are starting on, unless the prospect raises an objection first. Passive questions such as 'What did you think?', 'What are your thoughts?' or 'How are you feeling about everything?' may be used conversationally but do not count. Report yes when the rep's own speech makes that assumptive move; no when the rep waited for the prospect to direct the process or defaulted to a passive question; 'not_applicable' when the video never played or the prospect objected before the rep could reasonably assume the sale. A verified assumptive move satisfies the close requirement exactly like a direct commitment ask: it is a real attempt to close, a no_close_attempt event is forbidden when one exists, and it supports the adequate and strong close floors together with the other close signals. Keep direct_commitment_ask under its existing contract; report the assumptive move separately here.
6. Re-establish value when an objection appears. On objection the rep does not jump to payment plans or logistics. The rep first reconnects the prospect to why they applied or wanted to be on the show, ideally getting the prospect to say the value in their own words. Report value_reestablished yes/no and prospect_verbalized_value yes/no, both 'not_applicable' when no genuine objection occurred.
7. Prospect-specific urgency. After value, legitimate urgency tied to this prospect: their stated goal, timeline, business stage, the opportunity cost of waiting, or a real stated deadline such as the casting cohort closing at the end of the week. Credit only urgency connected to something the prospect said or to a real stated deadline. The weekly casting cohort closing (Sunday night) is a real company deadline: using it counts as legitimate urgency, and it is stronger when the rep connects it to the prospect's own timeline. Generic pressure or invented scarcity (fake limited spots, invented price increases) is never credited and is never a strength. Statuses are exactly yes, no, not_applicable or unable_to_determine; a weak or generic attempt is 'no'. Report yes/no, or 'not_applicable' when no objection or hesitation gave the rep a reason to create urgency.
8. Payment solutions presented appropriately. When affordability, cash flow or payment structure is the remaining blocker, the rep may introduce payment solutions, normally one at a time and no more than two or three before reassessing. The preferred order is value, then urgency, then payment solution. List every distinct payment structure the rep named (pay in full, four payments, three payments, two payments, deposit then plan) as payment_options with the timestamp where each was first named; restating the same structure is not a new option. Set offered_before_value true when the rep answered a financial objection with payment options before any attempt to re-establish value. Never penalize a rep who presented one option that the prospect accepted. Report payment_options_appropriate yes/no, or 'not_applicable' when no payment discussion beyond stating the price occurred.
9. Complete the handoff. When the prospect enrolls the rep explains the next steps: payment or deposit completion, the agreement, booking or confirming the onboarding call, and that a welcome or onboarding email is coming. Report onboarding_call_booked yes/no and welcome_email_explained yes/no; both are 'not_applicable' when the prospect did not move forward on this call.

How the procedure informs the dimensions (code applies deterministic ceilings; state the facts, do not do the arithmetic):
- Frame and control: missing disclosure, an unnecessarily long greenlight, or a hesitant transition into the video lower this dimension even when the conversation was friendly.
- Prospect read and tailoring: connecting an objection back to why the prospect applied, getting the prospect to verbalize value, and urgency tied to their goals are the tailoring behaviors that earn strong.
- Objection handling: the sequence identify or isolate, value, urgency, payment solution, resolution check is the standard. Jumping straight to payment options, or dumping many options, lowers this dimension.
- Close mechanics and momentum: failure to assume the sale after the video materially lowers this dimension. Assuming the sale or making a direct ask, plus an agreed specific next step, is at least adequate; adding a payment or agreement path is at least strong; confirmed payment, agreement and onboarding handoff is exemplary.
