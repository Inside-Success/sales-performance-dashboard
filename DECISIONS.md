# Project decisions

- **Script Adherence is a separate manager feature, with a limited pilot before the one-week backfill.** Preserve coaching, scoring, compliance and recovery behavior. Cover the two Zoom workspaces' configured closer groups; the owner maintains membership. Decided by Moonis, 2026-11-02.
- **Complete means at least 50% of the defined action, with evidence gaps excluded from the score.** Keep supporting components and source quotes internally, simple labels in the UI, flexible order, and script-specific video identities. This is adherence measurement, not proof of sales effectiveness. Decided by Moonis, confirmed 2026-11-02.
- **The project has a $40 maximum API budget and uses owner-approved credentials.** Reserve before submission, retain uncertain charges, and reconcile provider usage including cache writes and reasoning. Pilot comparisons do not establish production quality. Decided by Moonis, 2026-11-02.
- **Ongoing processing should use two scheduled cycles per day, not one n8n execution per call.** Provider batch state must survive between cycles. Full recurring rollout and the seven-complete-Miami-day backfill remain gated on this pilot's evidence. Decided by Moonis, confirmed 2026-11-02.
