Status: isolated experiment in progress. No production release is authorized by this document.

Mike's instruction is explicit: accept ACH/wire, but ask for the applicable down payment today to lock the buyer in. Do not merely wait for the transfer. ## Evaluation design

- Six common real calls: two per offer family, identical full sources and candidate instructions for Terra, Luna, Haiku and Sonnet writers, each with Terra review. This tests writer allocation while holding the reviewer constant; it is not every possible combination.
- Compare against both prior-lab Terra/Terra and historical production reports. Neither is ground truth.
- Eight additional previously untested records: two main documentary, two reality and four Next Level CEO calls. The NLCEO records were selected by descending date from a read-only source query, excluding the earlier 40-source pool, before observing candidate outputs. Actual dialogue, not titles, determines eligibility.
- Nine known difficult cases are regression material, not a fresh holdout. Four existing synthetic ACH/wire boundaries are also regression probes, not real-call accuracy evidence.
- Anonymous comparison uses full transcripts and source-based findings. Model votes are advisory; specific claims must be adjudicated. Different reasonable lessons are not errors. No tuning against the final validation results while calling them untouched.
- The original $40 ledger remains authoritative; $15.49505050 was already settled before this phase. Every request is reserved before submission, settled from its usage receipt, and reused only by exact request identity. No replay of unresolved requests.

## Engineering boundaries

The experiment preserves the legacy production v2 contract. New isolated checks bind source, prompt, policy, writer receipt, reviewer receipt and exact reviewed content; a preview never represents delivery acceptance. Manual and official preview channels share the same accepted object. Prose repairs cover the new teaching fields but cannot change classifications, payment status, evidence, focus ownership or eligibility. A changed candidate requires fresh review.

Current production coaching and its safety/provider workflows remain unchanged. No Slack, dashboard or official/manual report publisher is connected to the lab. There is no local development server. The attended provider fixture has no schedule or production callback, and is deactivated after all paid requests are reconciled.

## Release interpretation

The goal is enough isolated evidence for a direct replacement decision, not an automatic recommendation for another live pilot. Passing tests, matching old outputs or winning model votes does not by itself prove usefulness, complete production integration or sales uplift. The final decision must state any demonstrated faults and any untested integration boundary specifically; it must not promise perfection or invent hypothetical defects.

Results and recommendation will be added after the matched comparisons, validation and accounting finish.
