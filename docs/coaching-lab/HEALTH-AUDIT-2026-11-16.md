## Failure causes addressed

- Quotation and length errors receive bounded, separately accounted repairs, preserving the underlying report and material concerns rather than dropping objections.
- Temporarily unavailable governed knowledge receives at most two source-level retries. Unknown policy instructions and authorization failures still hold.
- Structural provider validation now includes internal teaching attempt references. It no longer treats a valid referenced turn as missing merely because that turn appears only in the internal teaching basis.
- Reviewer transport validation derives permitted item fields from the report schema. This fixes valid resolution edits being reported as failed checkpoints, while rejecting protected identities and unknown fields. The core still checks exact before-values, source evidence, substantive justification and the resulting report.
- A real shortening response proposed edits to unrequested fields. The formatting boundary now leaves those fields exactly unchanged, accepts only allowed patches with strict before-value and duplicate checks, records ignored paths, and still requires full review and safety. It does not drop objections or retry the paid writer.
- The concise presentation is selected explicitly. November 12 reports retain their historical reader after the generation label changes.

Retained failed provider checkpoints are historical evidence, not a count of missing reports. In particular, the recovered production call delivered despite two structural checkpoint errors; both mismatches were reproduced from its retained receipts and corrected. No paid provider requests were replayed to fix those labels.
