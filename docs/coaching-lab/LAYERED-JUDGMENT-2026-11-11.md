| Layer | Responsibility | What must not change it implicitly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations | Commercial purpose, legitimate initiative, persuasion, buyer agency, useful learning | A formatting rule, isolated example, reviewer caution or offer update |
| Interpretation | Full chronology; resource/access/allocation/value distinctions; buyer goals; supported uncertainty | Category labels, missing prior-call context, outcome alone |
| Strategy | Compare truthful selling routes, alternatives, commitment and continuation | Mandatory sequencing or assuming another defensible approach proves the rep wrong |
| Execution | Concrete teaching, actual words/actions, reviewer reasoning and limited repair | Cosmetic rewrites or an exhaustive compliance inventory |
