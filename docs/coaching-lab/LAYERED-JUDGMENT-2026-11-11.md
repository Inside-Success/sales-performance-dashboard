## Decision and next engineering target

Keep the production system unchanged. The next useful change is not a larger blind test run or a wholesale reviewer replacement:

1. Separate governed offer facts from embedded compliance-evaluator commands in the source pack. The bound prompt currently includes commands such as “Compliance: ... flag it”, despite the outer coaching instructions assigning auditing elsewhere. That mixing is observable; its causal contribution to an individual report is not proven.
2. Make the reviewer's distinction between handling quality and buyer resolution explicit in the judgment contract. In Mark's report, it changed sound handling to needs-work because the buyer's position remained unresolved. Its other edits correctly removed further selling after an explicit refusal and an unsupported criticism of earlier discovery. Preserve those benefits rather than bypassing review.
3. Compare teaching priority and justified criticism on new sources after a new frozen version. Do not tune the current held-out reports until they agree with a desired verdict. The Carmen and Michael examples show that grounded details can still displace more useful commercial teaching.
4. Finish authenticated preview/API checks when Vercel can provision the preview. No production cutover is justified by local build success.

The two historical NLCEO checks are explicitly not fresh NLCEO holdouts. Phil's candidate adds a direct commitment question before assuming a wait and correctly credits the subsequent adviser-review response. Diva's candidate distinguishes episode viewing from trailer promotion in the context of her exposure question and preserves her requested review. These do not establish company-wide accuracy or conversion impact.
