## Problem and change

The previous analyst → writer → reviewer → safety path could discard useful contextual teaching after writing. Accountability proved where edits came from, but not that the judgment was right. The opt-in `decision-plan-v1` path now uses four normal calls: Terra builds source-bound decisions and observations; Terra independently reviews those decisions; Sonnet expresses the reviewed plan through prose-only patches; DeepSeek verifies facts, safety and preservation of meaning against both the full source and reviewed plan.

The plan reuses the existing report ontology for decisions, evidence and inventory; it is not a new user-facing section. The writer cannot change categories, handling, resolution, evidence, inventory or order. It can hold a contradictory plan instead of silently changing it. Final verification may still identify a genuine error in the plan: it is not unquestionable ground truth. One bounded substantive prose correction is allowed, followed by another independent check; non-prose contradictions and repeat failures remain held. Existing source identity, provider receipts, review provenance and publication guards remain in force. Production defaults are unchanged.

Prompt responsibilities are separated: foundations/interpretation/strategy/execution and governed offer facts inform planning and judgment review; expression owns readability and concision; final verification owns factual integrity and meaning preservation. Existing production layouts/readers and report taxonomy stay intact. No new AI stage is added to the four-call candidate.

## Honest quality assessment

The architecture is cleaner: substantive review now precedes writing, and the final checker can see both the reviewed meaning and original evidence. Real reports averaged 549 words (median 478; range 349–1,064). The six paired regressions fell from 3,913 to 3,231 words, about 17% shorter. The longest fresh call still produces a long report because it contains multiple distinct consequential issues; brevity is improved, not guaranteed.

Useful results include legitimate direct-payment asks being credited, informed refusals respected, mixed handling/recovery retained, agreed/promised next steps distinguished, and payment links not mistaken for payment. The wire contrast applies Mike's first-payment requirement without inventing a universal $4,500 deposit or asking for a second deposit when the initial payment was already made. The recap contrast preserves payment uncertainty, the useful recap/receipt check and the rep's recovery without inventing incapacity. Several reports are comparable rather than superior to production; production sometimes selects a useful lesson the candidate omits. That alone is not a failure.

**One substantive failure remains on fresh call 9586.** At T79/T81 the buyer asks for a copy of the licensing details so he can retain and review the information. The final report collapses his needs into general adviser review, losing this concrete information need. Production retained it. A separate objection entry is not mandatory, but preserving that need and its unresolved handling somewhere is important. This is not a demand for a specific phrase or category.

The omission starts in the planner's observations, before the writer. The judgment reviewer does not restore it, and a meaning-preservation check cannot preserve a decision that never reached the plan. The report's tax/VIP corrections and mission-based teaching remain useful, but those strengths do not cancel the omission. This identifies a source-to-plan coverage weakness rather than another formatting or output-style issue. No further semantic tuning or paid cohort was started after this finding.

Recommendation: retain the implemented stage boundaries, source/knowledge handoff fixes and verified adapters, but **hold production replacement**. The next bounded change should make source-to-plan coverage independently auditable: identify decision-relevant buyer requests/constraints from the source before comparing the selected plan, then explicitly retain, combine or reject them with evidence. Do not force every routine question into an objection or hard-code this call's desired answer. Evaluate that change with the existing contrasting cases plus a small unused set; do not restart a broad model tournament or keep polishing the writer. This is a remaining recommendation, not implemented or proven remediation.
