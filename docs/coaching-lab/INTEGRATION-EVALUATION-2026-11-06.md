Status: isolated implementation and comparison saved in draft PR #391, stacked on #390. **Not a production replacement or release recommendation.** No production workflow or accepted report was changed.

## Outcome

The new direction produces more useful, specific teaching in several cases, but is not uniformly better than existing coaching. Sonnet 5.5 writer + Terra reviewer is the preferred development candidate for readability and grouping related lessons. Terra + Terra costs less and often produces good teaching, but the long fresh reality call exposed a substantive false criticism that survived its initial review. Neither configuration earned an unattended-release verdict.

Tyler's pinned source prompt and layered foundation remain the base, with the documented seven adaptations and governed three-family offer context. Mike's offer-specific ACH/wire down-payment instruction remains included. The new shared duties compare useful positive contributions with buyer-handling and execution lessons, check material offer representations even without a buyer objection, and distinguish genuine statements from quoted/hypothetical dialogue. These strengthen the existing writer/reviewer stages; no additional normal coaching AI stage is introduced.

## What was implemented

- `coaching-foundations-v3` contract and canonical renderer, alongside unchanged legacy readers. One home per lesson; objections near the top; distinct repeatable strengths last; pending promises separate from new recommendations.
- Source/policy/request/digest binding, consistent review verdicts, explicit eligibility holds, strict field validation and immutable evidence/classification boundaries.
- Atomic whole-field prose repairs invalidate the candidate's previous safety decision. Publication envelopes require a matching safety decision and exact official/manual source identity.
- Shared candidate readers in official ingestion, manual ingestion, dashboard cards and report chat. Chat receives the matching policy and full transcript. These are isolated branch changes, not live readers.
- An additive offline OpenAI adapter for the existing durable provider. It preserves owner leases, cache restoration, failure paths and no-blind-retry behavior. The generated graph was compiled and its old branches compared, but **was not applied to the provider workflow**.

## Evaluation and honest findings

Thirty paired report attempts covered twelve distinct real calls. The first frozen revision produced eighteen reports on nine calls. Three of those sources were new at first use and subsequently became development material. A general commercial-accuracy duty was then tested in twelve report attempts on six calls, including two additional fresh sources selected before generating the outputs. Older NLCEO material was included as regression coverage; there was no fresh NLCEO validation in this phase. This follow-up assessment was not blinded, and it is not a statistical accuracy estimate.

Observed improvements:

- Both configurations preserved the distinction between wanting advice, requiring approval, needing payment time, and genuinely lacking funds. They credited reasonable direct commitment asks instead of inventing a compulsory extra discovery question.
- The first revision missed consequential offer statements on two fresh reality/mixed-offer calls. The commercial-duty revision caught conditional VIP protection being presented too broadly, unsupported celebrity/portrayal expectations, a documentary Lite podcast being promised, and guaranteed future reality selection. Existing production also missed some of these; on the mixed-offer call it had already caught the casting promise, so this is not a clean sweep over production.
- Positive teaching remained meaningful: connecting a buyer's own story to the assets, testing a practical use, and making a reasonable direct ask were retained rather than forcing criticism.
- On the new documentary source, both configurations distinguished outside-platform submission from placement and licensed reuse from ownership. Sonnet grouped the report more coherently; Terra added an optional closing alternative that was not necessarily needed.

Failures and limits retained in the record:

1. On the long fresh reality call, Terra interpreted a buyer's joking imitation of the rep ending the conversation as a real request to leave. The surrounding exchange shows the rep describing how he would behave **if** bored. The resulting criticism was unsupported.
2. The Sonnet/Terra version of that call was held because its corrected report omitted `counterevidence_ids` on its final positive lesson. All listed references existed, but omission is not the same as an explicit empty array. The validator was not weakened and the rejected output was preserved.
3. Both configurations still produced long, granular reports on the complex call. Some coverage varied across review attempts, including a promise to provide materials. Technical acceptance does not establish complete coverage or optimal editorial priority.

A general contextual-attribution and complete-schema instruction was added to the existing reviewer, then three saved drafts were re-reviewed: both configurations for the difficult reality call and one positive documentary control. All three passed structure; the unsupported time-limit criticism was removed, the missing field was supplied explicitly, and the positive control remained intact. These are **known-case regressions**, not untouched validation. No new draft was purchased for these checks. This demonstrates a useful correction, not proof that all similar errors are eliminated.

The current ACH/wire trigger was correctly distinguished from simply mentioning payment methods on the fresh call: the buyer did not choose wire/ACH, so no missed-deposit finding was justified. Earlier four synthetic ACH/wire probes remain historical evidence; they were not counted as new real-call validation here.
