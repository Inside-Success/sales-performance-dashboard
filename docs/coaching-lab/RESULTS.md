# Isolated coaching evaluation — November 5–6, 2026

Latest isolated phase: [Tyler-faithful evaluation](TYLER-FAITHFUL-EVALUATION.md). Earlier results below remain historical evidence, not the latest candidate verdict.

Status: isolated evaluation complete. Candidate selected; not ready for unattended production activation.

## What was built

An attended, separate evaluation harness with versioned source layers, full-transcript inputs, two-stage coaching, explicit factual review, source-bound evidence and deterministic reader reports. Objection-specific teaching appears with the objection; distinct transferable lessons appear afterward. A main takeaway points to an existing lesson instead of creating a repeated summary. Production consumers and workflows have not been replaced.

The foundation snapshots Tyler's design, current definitions, governed offer knowledge, dated updates and the report contract as separately identifiable layers. Four reasoning layers do not require four model calls. Recorded conversation content is evidence, not authority to override the governed offer rules.

## Fairness and limits

- Frozen 36-source corpus: 14 main documentaries, 8 Next Level CEO, 14 reality; 24 development and 12 held out, four held-out per family. These are report-bearing historical sources, not a random sample of every Zoom call or a source-coverage audit.
- Six balanced development calls compare integrated coaching/review against analyst/composer using the same Sonnet/Opus pair. The analyst arm's output-limit failures and larger-budget recoveries remain counted.
- The same six compare lower-cost integrated configurations. Haiku's original high-reasoning 12,000-output-token setup failed twice; the medium-reasoning 24,000 setup is separately identified. This compares usable configurations, not abstract model intelligence at identical settings.
- Terra/Terra and Haiku-medium/Terra were frozen as finalists before opening the twelve held-out calls. Coaching prompts and knowledge were not tuned on those outputs. Holdout selection is not a subsequent independent accuracy guarantee.
- Anonymous, randomized report comparisons use full transcripts and include historical production reports. Judge findings are checked against the source: judges can misread chronology or mistake a different useful lesson for an error. Votes and writer/reviewer agreement are not accuracy metrics.
- Complete independent transcript reads cover the six development screen calls and six held-out calls; other held-out cases receive targeted source verification. One additional development transcript was independently read in full before its output; the other expanded cases received targeted source verification and every selected final report was read. Remaining development outputs are an extension check, not additional untouched validation. Review coverage is recorded privately rather than implying every source was independently read in full.
- Synthetic ACH/wire probes test current versus historical authority and payment-state boundaries. They do not count as real-call accuracy or establish every payment scenario.
- Production's earlier delivery failures, manual/official recovery and source deduplication remain separate. A valid JSON report is not proof of delivery, rep usefulness or increased sales.

## Operational findings already established

The analyst/composer variant needed larger output limits and retained unsupported statements in a recovered example. The integrated design preserves an explicit independent review and is the preferred architecture from this screen. A held-out fragmented recording exposed writer/reviewer eligibility disagreement; the contract held it rather than silently accepting the reviewer's change. A safe hold is not a completed report.

## Candidate decision

Prefer integrated Terra coaching followed by Terra substantive review for the next integration trial. Haiku-medium/Terra remains a credible cheaper alternative. In the twelve anonymous holdout comparisons the judge preferred Terra over Haiku five times, Haiku once and tied six times. These are preference counts, not accuracy percentages; one Haiku comparison is a proposed output rejected by the eligibility contract.

Source review supports Terra's advantage on useful secondary lessons and chronology: interest versus credit costs, the buyer reopening a funding question, and an ambiguous readiness signal. Haiku more precisely explained the wrong-offer correction in another case. Both new configurations sometimes miss a useful point retained by the existing report, notably clear written payment amounts and dates. Historical reports also contain actual quote/chronology errors and repeated or unnecessarily prescriptive coaching. There is no evidence of uniform superiority or proven sales uplift.

The fragmented-source case needs a delivery hold even though Terra's object validates structurally. A coherent snippet does not establish a substantive buyer conversation. Other findings are calibration issues: handling labels can be harsher than the prose warrants, and a recommended verification can occasionally be blended into an agreed next step. Preserve these as evaluation findings rather than silently rewriting outputs and counting the edited examples as model success.

## Recommendation

Keep the integrated two-stage architecture and Terra/Terra as the leading candidate. Do **not** activate it unattended yet. The next bounded pass should:

1. Separate dated mandatory commercial requirements from optional sales techniques, and explicitly test the first-payment/lock-in rule across current and historical examples.
2. Make uncertain source eligibility a delivery hold even when both models agree on a narrow lesson; tighten next-step/handling consistency without adding another routine AI stage.
3. Verify the new contract through isolated manual and official paths, including durable OpenAI transport, cached reuse, safety/repair mappings, old report compatibility and authenticated rendering. Then seek approval for a small live canary with delivery receipts and rollback.

Use fresh validation examples for a changed prompt; do not repeatedly tune to these twelve holdout results and then claim independent accuracy. No broad rewrite or additional model tournament is justified by this evidence. The design is promising and materially cheaper in this experiment, but improved rep outcomes and company-wide reliability require subsequent evidence.
