## Quality assessment

The 11 paired reports fell from 8,149 to 7,323 words in total (10.1% shorter). Nine shortened and two lengthened; no hard truncation was used. The range across all 13 is 473–1,056 words. The longest fresh report has four genuine concerns and several distinct consequential promises, so it is not evidence that every report fits the preferred 450–750-word guide.

Most reviewed examples preserve fair credit, genuine concerns, payment qualifications and actual commitments while dropping low-value wording criticism. The fresh documentary case respects a final refusal, retains the request for participant input, and credits a legitimate lower-tier offer. The fresh reality case retains distribution uncertainty, the unanswered refund question, joint funding authority and the guest-access issue; it distinguishes promised emails from delivery and does not falsely trigger the ACH/wire rule. These are manual source-review observations, not a measured accuracy percentage.

One known regression remains: call 9539. The source analyst correctly preserves the buyer explicitly forgetting the conversation and the changing state of attention. The reviewer dismisses this because it does not prove incapacity. The final report still correctly says ability is not consent and verifies the previous payment before a new charge, but loses the useful recap/check/reconnect teaching that the previous selected-B report retained. The concern is practical relevance to the sales interaction, not a requirement to mention alcohol, diagnose the buyer, or criticize a direct answer.

**Recommendation: retain the selected architecture and the presentation improvements, but do not call this entire candidate ready for unattended production replacement yet.** The remaining issue is reviewer judgment about when uncertain context changes the appropriate next move. It is not missing source data, a renderer failure or a reason to restart a broad model tournament. A further correction should address that responsibility generally and be evaluated against both actual attention-loss cases and ordinary distractions/direct questions; do not prescribe this one call's desired answer. No additional paid cohort was started after this frozen evaluation.
