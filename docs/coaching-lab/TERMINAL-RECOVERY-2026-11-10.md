## Changes

- Contract validation reports all detected faults with locations in one pass. The existing single repair sees every fault; independent factual review and safety remain required. No generic filler, relaxed validation, unlimited retry or additional routine model stage.
- A reviewed exclusion has its own source/policy/evidence/digest/paid-receipt validation and maps to the existing official exclusion receipt or manual refusal callback. It cannot create a score, coaching report, Doc or report notification.
- Exact retained first-stage writer/reviewer contract failures can resume into their bounded repair without purchasing the original request again. Their checkpoints remain failed, never approved; reuse has zero new cost. Unknown outcomes, refusal/truncation and repeated repair failures do not gain replay permission.
- Scoped workflow patch preserves all connections, node inventories, credentials and unrelated settings. A new core is used; the previous core stays available for in-flight executions and rollback. Historical reports, prompts, report layout, scoring, compliance, FAQ and adherence remain unchanged.
