# Ask Sales release verification — September 16, 2026

Status: authorized rollout in progress; production remains V5.14 / DeepSeek pending quality and hosted verification. No production or n8n mutation has been performed in this release phase.

## Scope

Only Ask Sales runtime, knowledge/admin review, feedback authorization and its knowledge collectors are changed. Coaching, report chat, sales-impact chat, compliance and scoring are preserved. Shared DeepSeek credentials must remain because other features use them. The new Ask Sales path uses Luna with no automatic provider fallback; old code remains available for rollback without running alongside it.

## Final implementation

Every turn receives contextual query planning, product-scoped retrieval, a natural draft and grounded review. Luna uses low reasoning for planning/drafting and medium for review. Three calls are bounded by per-call timeouts. Reviewed source records have a separate retrieval ranking; fragments from one source cannot crowd out other sources. Explicit governing-record relationships preserve approved source decisions. No question-specific answer override was added.

86 reviewed additions cover reality, HubSpot, current operations, objection coaching, package deliverables and corrected historical summaries. In particular: Money Mondays was explicitly corrected as active after a mistaken cancellation report; a partner being signed up for onboarding did not establish contract-signing authority; common deliverables across regular shows did not mean identical license tiers. Show-list and due-diligence resources have usable source URLs. Mandatory post-Call-1 email/text instructions are separate from day-before reminders. Current dates and product scope are retained.

49 of 50 registered refresh sources are enabled. The individual Green Light client-document tracking sheet is disabled as a general policy source. No source documents or Slack messages were changed. Collector patches cover allowlists and payload-retention settings on the two collectors plus their orchestrator and analyzer; applying/publishing them remains a coordinated release step.

## Limits

Source citations prove reference membership, not entailment. Model review is fallible. Contract-dependent decisions remain bounded; signed agreements are not rewritten by knowledge refresh. Two audio Slack posts lacked readable transcripts through MCP. Some missing live-account data, specific contract details and unpublished schedules genuinely require the responsible team. All quality labels are agent review, not independent human acceptance or a promise of perfect answers.
