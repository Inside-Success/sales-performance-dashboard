# Ask Sales revamp acceptance — September 16, 2026

This records the reviewed release candidate and acceptance evidence. The production deployment and active collector receipts are recorded in [dashboard PR195](https://github.com/Inside-Success/sales-performance-dashboard/pull/195) and the paired [FAQ PR75](https://github.com/Inside-Success/faq-chatbot/pull/75). Earlier September 15 reports are historical checkpoints, not current-state claims.

## What changed

Ask Sales now uses contextual query planning, product-scoped multi-query retrieval, a natural answer draft and grounded review. GPT-5.6 Luna uses **medium reasoning for all three stages**, with bounded per-call timeouts and no automatic DeepSeek fallback. General sales advice and ordinary conversation may use general knowledge; company prices, permissions, promises and procedures require company evidence. A new-subject boundary keeps a previous lead's personal facts out of the next lead's answer. The API supplies the latest question once and retains bounded prior conversation context.

Reviewed records have an independent retrieval ranking, source fragments cannot crowd out every other source, and explicit governing relationships connect old guidance to applicable current decisions. This is not a bank of question-specific answer overrides. Optional internal routing labels are retained only when they exactly match supplied evidence; invalid labels are discarded without rewriting supported prose. Source-reference and URL checks still apply. The discarded-label event is recorded for diagnostics. Source membership is not proof that a model interpreted a source correctly.

The registry contains **87 reviewed additions/corrections**, including reality-show rules and linked material, HubSpot, Bill.com commissions, objection coaching, package deliverables, wire-payment handling, qualification, DNC, show availability, Money Mondays and the separate pre-/post-Call-1 emails. Current effective knowledge version: `b093bfd070017710ec873413`.

Runtime, admin review, release preview, publication manifests and health share the effective registry. Feedback checks enforce message ownership. Unanswered questions appear in admin review; fabricated numerical confidence is not shown for unscored revamp answers. **49 of 50 refresh sources** are enabled; the individual Green Light client-document tracking sheet is excluded as general policy. Collector patches expand the allowlists and prevent raw execution-payload persistence on the two collectors, orchestrator and analyzer. Source ingestion redacts credentials while retaining useful policy links. No source documents or Slack messages were changed.

Coaching, report chat, sales-impact chat, scoring and compliance are outside the implementation scope. The shared DeepSeek key remains necessary for other features and the knowledge analyzer. Old Ask Sales code remains available for rollback but is not a parallel paid execution path when the revamp selector is active.

## What the comparison supports

Real questions about money objections, “need to think about it,” commission invoicing and startup/non-owner eligibility that previously received generic Slack referrals now receive useful advice or supported procedures. A Friday wire-payment question now distinguishes onboarding from confirmed receipt and PayMe. The bot also avoids the old false inference that a show listed for casting is necessarily already watchable. Questions the older version answered correctly, such as debit-card acceptance and standard pricing, remain supported.

These are reviewed examples, not a measured accuracy percentage. The result is meaningfully more useful than the earlier rigid system, but not perfect or proven on every possible wording. Generalization comes from the architecture and source work, not just changing the model. The earlier 40-case model comparison favored Luna's structured-output reliability over DeepSeek (40 versus 38 technical completions); it predates this final medium-reasoning candidate and should not be called a controlled final-model comparison.

## Remaining limitations

- Some ambiguous questions still receive an unnecessary clarification, an incomplete product comparison, or an irrelevant caveat. One TEDx identity follow-up was misinterpreted; a broad refund comparison omitted known reality guidance, while the explicit reality refund check retrieved it. These are retained quality limitations, not relabeled passes.
- The reality PDF versus mandatory Call 2 documentary-benefit discrepancy is not formally resolved. Current selling guidance follows the newer video placing the full interview in VIP; existing signed agreements remain controlling.
- Historical policy summaries can still be ambiguous. Models can misinterpret supported records, and a review model is not an independent human validator. Missing contract/account facts, dates and authority require genuine clarification or an appropriate team.
- One linked Google source was inaccessible and two new Slack audio posts had no readable MCP transcript. Manual coverage is substantial, not exhaustive. Newly linked videos are not automatically discovered/transcribed; periodic linked-source review remains necessary.
- Provider outages/timeouts remain possible. They are displayed as technical failures rather than falsely claiming missing company knowledge or sending users to Slack.

## Deployment and rollback

Use a fresh production build with production DB/auth, never promote the isolated preview environment. Set only the Ask Sales provider/model/selector and server-only OpenAI key. Apply the four scoped collector patches after production ingestion safeguards are active, explicitly publish them and verify the active graphs/settings. Do not manually trigger a workflow that could send Slack notifications.

Prefer rollback by selecting V5.14 on the current code and rebuilding, retaining ingestion privacy changes. Reverting to the old deployment instead requires restoring the old collector allowlists first. Preserve candidate ledgers and diagnostics, pause publication while rolling back, and never write the candidate ledger into the legacy registry. See `rollout/README.md` in the dashboard for the procedure and the PR receipts for actual deployed revisions.
