# Editorial responsibility — 2026-11-11

This is the final expression layer, after source interpretation and sales judgment. It cannot change approved offer facts, conceal a material buyer concern or change what happened. The analyst's inventory is not a quota of report sections. The writer selects useful teaching; the reviewer checks both justified omissions and unnecessary criticism against the full interaction.

Before adding a correction, identify the actual behavioral defect and what the rep can do differently. A merely more precise paraphrase, possible ambiguity not present in context, or reviewer-preferred wording is not a defect. Interpret ordinary conversational language in context before alleging a claim. Conversely, a consequential false promise about the product, payment or buyer's rights warrants correction even without proof that it changed the outcome. Keep the correction proportionate; do not make easily checked policy details the main lesson when buyer understanding or selling judgment offers more value.

Give each insight one home. Keep every distinct material buyer concern visible, but do not retell the same concern in a lesson or repeat its assessment in the teaching and example. When several errors teach the same principle, combine them in one grounded lesson with concrete evidence rather than a list of near-identical critiques. Preserve useful initiative and later recovery when explaining a mixed response.

Write for a rep who will use this on the next call. An assessment identifies the actual issue; teaching gives a practical move and why it helps; an optional example adds usable wording only when it teaches something the explanation does not already provide. Prefer one short sentence per role and remove filler before removing nuance. Use short paragraphs, plain language and specific actions. Usually 450–750 report words are enough; a complex call may need more. This is an editorial guide, never a truncation rule, required minimum, or reason to omit material concerns, qualified payment outcomes, meaningful commitments or useful strengths.

Select lessons for their transferable value, not to fill each reasoning layer. Keep layer tags internally for traceability. Corrections and useful alternatives precede strengths. Preserve the distinction between agreed, promised and suggested next steps, and reconcile them with the actual ending. A reviewer may remove low-value or repetitive material with a source-grounded reason; never remove the only explanation of a consequential problem merely to shorten the report.
