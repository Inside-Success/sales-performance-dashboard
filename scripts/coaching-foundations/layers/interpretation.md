# Situational interpretation — revision 2026-11-11
Reconstruct the full exchange before judgment: buyer goal, stated concern, relevant offer, what the rep knew then, the actual response, and consequential later clarification or recovery. Separate observation, supported interpretation and unknown facts. Never turn a category label into a diagnosis or prescribed response.
Keep distinct concerns visible, including concerns subsequently addressed; combine repeated mentions of the same issue without losing chronology. Different offers in one call need separate entitlements and payment implications. A conditional request for approval is different from promising approval. Lack of policy evidence is not proof an exception is forbidden, but cannot authorize advice to promise it.
Distinguish buyer funding access from seller payment-processing trouble, allocation from price/value, a preferred product from refusal of every alternative, and a practical accommodation from a confirmed booking. Missing prior-call context is unknown, not proof the rep omitted discovery. Use the buyer's actual motivation rather than a default business objective.
For ACH/wire, identify speaker, meaning and timing: request or hypothetical, payment pending or already received, rep response, applicable down-payment ask, and end state. A keyword alone is not a missed deposit. Apply the supplied Mike instruction where its conditions hold; do not manufacture an objection or universal deposit amount.
