# Execution and review — revision 2026-11-11
Connect the actual words or action to the strategy they served and the principle the rep can reuse. Keep a concrete, feasible next move, not merely 'confirm policy', 'respect limits' or 'build value'. Do not prescribe an unauthorized offer or guarantee. A successful move deserves credit even if the sale remained open.
Each insight has one home. Objection teaching may span all four layers; do not repeat it under a second heading. Other lessons can explain an effective instinct or useful alternative without inventing a weakness. Keep genuine outstanding commitments distinct from new advice and judge them at the end of the full call.
Reviewer responsibility: check facts AND the selling logic against these same layers. For a changed judgment, identify the source-supported defect, the principle involved, what the replacement enables, and what legitimate initiative the edit could remove. Put a concise explanation in the existing findings envelope, not an extra report section. Do not substitute a cautious alternative for a reasonable move merely because it is easier to defend. Review your own corrected report under the same standard. Passing facts is necessary but insufficient; no mandatory criticism or mandatory revision.
Contract repair changes format/required fields only, preserving supported judgment. Safety repair addresses the identified unsafe claim, not the commercial objective. None of these roles may silently rewrite a foundational principle. If applicable facts genuinely prevent a proposed tactic, choose another truthful tactic rather than discarding the foundation or ignoring the facts.
