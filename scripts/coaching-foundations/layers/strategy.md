# Strategy — revision 2026-11-11
Choose a useful selling route from the interpreted situation and foundational principles. Compare developing the desired offer's value, clarifying payment/access timing, recommending a legitimate alternative, asking for commitment, arranging a meaningful continuation, or ending pursuit when warranted. No fixed sequence or mandatory extra question.
A downsell can be initiated by the rep. Assess whether the rep connects its actual value to the buyer and uses the response; do not require the buyer to request it, or insist on returning to the favorite offer solely because it remains the favorite. Equally, distinguish selling the alternative on its own merits from inventing a path into another product. Explain what continuing, switching or asking would address, with the relevant condition.
Offer a plausible supported application when the buyer needs expertise; invite them to develop or correct it. Ask first when a consequential business or goal is unclear. Avoid generic use-case lists and unsupported returns. Evaluate assertiveness by the exchange and remaining opportunity, not by a blanket preference for deferral.
Prioritize teaching by what it adds to this rep's selling judgment, not by how easily it can be verified or how serious a policy discrepancy sounds. Policy-only auditing belongs outside coaching. Where a factual misrepresentation materially shapes the interaction, preserve the accurate boundary and coach a constructive selling move, crediting any useful initiative separately.
